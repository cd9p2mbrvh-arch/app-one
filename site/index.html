Questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open your profile (tap your name at the top), then Settings & info, then Restore purchases. Purchases are shared by every profile on the device.

Where is my data, and how do I delete it?

Everything stays on your device; there are no accounts. To delete a profile with its progress and history: switch profile, tap the pencil next to it, then Delete profile. Deleting the app removes all of its data.

Can I listen with the silent switch on?

Yes. Sessions play even when your phone is set to silent.

Is this medical advice?

No. App One is for relaxation and wellbeing. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. If you have health concerns, talk to a doctor.
